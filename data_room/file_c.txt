LIABILITY CAP, INDEMNIFICATION, AND RISK ALLOCATION PROVISIONS

Agreement Reference: MSA-2024-0847-SUPP
Effective Date: January 15, 2024
Parties: Meridian Technologies Inc. ("Licensor") and Apex Capital Partners LLC ("Licensee")
This Supplement is incorporated into and forms part of PSL-2024-0847.

--- ARTICLE 1: AGGREGATE LIABILITY CAP ---

1.1 General Liability Cap

EXCEPT AS SET FORTH IN SECTION 1.2, THE TOTAL CUMULATIVE LIABILITY OF EITHER PARTY TO THE OTHER PARTY FOR ALL CLAIMS ARISING UNDER OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT (INCLUDING NEGLIGENCE AND STRICT LIABILITY), OR OTHERWISE, SHALL NOT EXCEED THE GREATER OF: (A) THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY LICENSEE TO LICENSOR UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM; OR (B) FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND UNITED STATES DOLLARS (USD $500,000).

1.2 Carve-Outs from Liability Cap — Uncapped Exposure Categories

The following categories of claims and damages are expressly excluded from the limitation of liability set forth in Section 1.1 and shall not be subject to any cap or ceiling:

(a) THIRD-PARTY INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY INFRINGEMENT CLAIMS: Any claims, losses, damages, judgments, costs, or expenses arising from or related to the actual or alleged infringement of any third party's patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right by the Software or by Licensor's technology as incorporated into the Software. For the avoidance of doubt, this carve-out applies to claims by open-source license holders, standards-essential patent holders, and all other intellectual property rights holders.

(b) GROSS NEGLIGENCE AND WILLFUL MISCONDUCT: Any claims arising from a party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, fraud, or intentional misrepresentation.

(c) BREACH OF CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS: Any claims arising from unauthorized disclosure of the other party's confidential information or trade secrets.

(d) BREACH OF DATA PROTECTION OBLIGATIONS: Any claims arising from violations of applicable data protection or privacy laws.

1.3 Interaction of Liability Cap with IP Warranty Provisions

The parties acknowledge that the IP warranties set forth in PSL-2024-0847 Article 1 are fundamental to the commercial basis of this Agreement. Accordingly, any breach of the IP warranties — including but not limited to any misrepresentation regarding the absence of copyleft-licensed components in the Software — shall be treated as a claim arising from third-party intellectual property infringement for purposes of Section 1.2(a), and shall therefore be excluded from the general liability cap.

--- ARTICLE 2: MUTUAL INDEMNIFICATION ---

2.1 Licensor's Indemnification Obligations

Subject to the terms and conditions of this Article 2, Licensor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Licensee and its affiliates, officers, directors, employees, and agents (collectively, "Licensee Indemnitees") from and against any and all third-party claims, actions, suits, proceedings, losses, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) ("Claims") to the extent arising from or related to:

(a) PROPRIETARY TECHNOLOGY CLAIMS: Any third-party claim that the Software, as delivered by Licensor and used by Licensee in accordance with this Agreement, infringes any third-party copyright, patent, trademark, or trade secret right, but only to the extent such claim arises from the proprietary components of the Software that are exclusively developed and owned by Licensor.

(b) LICENSOR'S GROSS NEGLIGENCE OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT: Any third-party claim arising from Licensor's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

(c) LICENSOR'S BREACH OF REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES: Any claim arising from Licensor's material breach of the representations and warranties set forth in PSL-2024-0847.

2.2 Explicit Exclusions from Licensor's Indemnification

NOTWITHSTANDING SECTION 2.1, LICENSOR SHALL HAVE NO OBLIGATION TO DEFEND, INDEMNIFY, OR HOLD HARMLESS LICENSEE INDEMNITEES FROM ANY CLAIM ARISING FROM OR RELATED TO:

(a) OPEN-SOURCE LICENSE VIOLATIONS: Any claim, demand, action, or proceeding brought by any open-source software licensor, open-source license rights holder, or any third party asserting rights under any open-source software license (including without limitation the GNU General Public License, GNU Affero General Public License, GNU Lesser General Public License, Mozilla Public License, Eclipse Public License, or any other open-source, free software, or copyleft license) arising from Licensor's failure to comply with the terms of such licenses in the Software.

(b) UPSTREAM DEPENDENCY LICENSE NON-COMPLIANCE: Any claim arising from the incorporation of third-party software components into the Software in a manner that violates the license terms applicable to such components, including any claim arising from the use of copyleft-licensed software in a manner incompatible with Licensor's commercial licensing model.

(c) LICENSOR'S FALSE OR MISLEADING REPRESENTATIONS: Any claim that results from or is exacerbated by Licensor's inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading representations regarding the Software's intellectual property composition, third-party dependencies, or open-source license status, where Licensee's reliance on such representations caused or contributed to Licensee's exposure to third-party claims.

For the avoidance of doubt, Section 2.2(c) creates a critical gap: if Licensor has made false IP representations (as warranted in PSL-2024-0847 Article 1) and those representations induced Licensee to enter this Agreement or to rely on Licensor's IP claims in its own downstream activities, Licensor is not obligated to indemnify Licensee for resulting third-party claims. This gap interacts directly with Section 1.2(a): Licensee's resulting third-party IP claims are uncapped, but Licensor's indemnification does not cover them if they stem from Licensor's open-source compliance failures.

2.3 Licensee's Indemnification Obligations

Licensee shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Licensor and its affiliates, officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any Claims arising from: (a) Licensee's use of the Software in violation of this Agreement; (b) Licensee's gross negligence or willful misconduct; or (c) Licensee's combination of the Software with third-party products or services not authorized by Licensor, but only to the extent such claims would not have arisen but for such combination.

--- ARTICLE 3: INDEMNIFICATION PROCEDURE ---

3.1 Notice and Cooperation

The indemnified party shall: (a) promptly notify the indemnifying party in writing of any Claim for which indemnification is sought, provided that failure to provide timely notice shall only relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations to the extent the indemnifying party is actually prejudiced by such failure; (b) grant the indemnifying party sole control over the defense and settlement of such Claim; and (c) provide reasonable cooperation in the defense of such Claim at the indemnifying party's expense.

3.2 Settlement

The indemnifying party shall not settle any Claim in a manner that: (a) imposes any obligation, restriction, or liability on the indemnified party; (b) requires the indemnified party to admit liability; or (c) would adversely affect the indemnified party's reputation or business operations, in each case without the prior written consent of the indemnified party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

--- ARTICLE 4: CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES WAIVER ---

4.1 Mutual Exclusion of Consequential Damages

EXCEPT AS SET FORTH IN SECTION 4.2, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, PUNITIVE, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES ARISING UNDER OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT, INCLUDING WITHOUT LIMITATION LOSS OF PROFITS, LOSS OF REVENUE, LOSS OF DATA, OR BUSINESS INTERRUPTION, EVEN IF SUCH PARTY HAS BEEN ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

4.2 Carve-Outs from Consequential Damages Waiver

The consequential damages waiver set forth in Section 4.1 shall not apply to:

(a) Damages arising from breach of confidentiality obligations;
(b) Damages arising from gross negligence or willful misconduct;
(c) Damages arising from third-party intellectual property infringement claims that fall within the uncapped exposure category under Section 1.2(a);
(d) Damages arising from open-source license violations where the violating party has been notified of the non-compliance and has failed to remediate within ninety (90) days of written notice.

--- ARTICLE 5: RISK ALLOCATION SUMMARY ---

5.1 Parties' Acknowledgment

The parties acknowledge that: (a) the limitations of liability and indemnification provisions set forth in this Agreement reflect a reasonable and negotiated allocation of risk between the parties; (b) the general liability cap of $500,000 under Section 1.1 is predicated on the accuracy of Licensor's IP representations and warranties in PSL-2024-0847; and (c) the uncapped exposure categories under Section 1.2 represent risks that the parties have determined cannot reasonably be subject to a monetary cap given the potentially unlimited nature of third-party intellectual property infringement liability.

5.2 Regulatory Compliance

Each party shall comply with all applicable laws and regulations in connection with its performance under this Agreement, including applicable export control, data protection, and intellectual property laws.

--- ARTICLE 6: DISPUTE RESOLUTION ---

6.1 Governing Law and Venue

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Any disputes shall be resolved exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.

6.2 Escalation Procedure

Prior to initiating litigation, the parties shall: (a) provide written notice of the dispute; (b) attempt good-faith executive-level negotiations for thirty (30) days; (c) if unresolved, submit to non-binding mediation before JAMS for an additional thirty (30) days.
